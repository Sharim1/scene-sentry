Data sources

Scene Sentry's catalog is built from third-party data. We are grateful to the communities that maintain it.

TheTVDB

Metadata provided by TheTVDB. Please consider adding missing information or subscribing. Most titles, descriptions, release dates and episode listings in our catalog come from TheTVDB.

TVmaze

Some TV show data, including episode information and the user ratings shown on some shows, comes from TVmaze and its contributors. It is used under the CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

Changes we make

We have modified this data: we reformat it, combine it with information from other sources, and fill gaps. These changes are ours, and are not endorsed by TheTVDB or TVmaze.

Images

Posters and other artwork are the property of their respective owners.

Copyright and takedown requests

If you own the rights to an image or other material shown on Scene Sentry and would like it removed, please contact us. Tell us which title and page it appears on and how to reach you, and we will look into it promptly.
